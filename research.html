Research

I am a fluid mechanician by training. My research connects small-scale fluid mechanics with soft matter, asking how interfaces and particle-scale structure govern transport.

Current research / Yale

Diffusion in crowded environments

I combine particle-tracking experiments, simulations, and statistical modeling to connect tracer diffusion with the geometry of crowded soft materials. Our work identifies accessible pore size as a key descriptor of transport, even when distinct microstructures produce similar mean-squared displacements.

Current research / Yale

Flow through deformable particle packings

I investigate the coupling between fluid flow and deformation in confined hydrogel particle packings. By comparing particles of different stiffness and soft–rigid mixtures, I examine how deformation and rearrangement influence pressure drop, permeability, and clogging.

Previous research

Nanobubbles & colloidal stability

I studied the generation and stability of bulk nanobubbles, connecting surfactant chemistry and solution conditions to size and surface charge. Related work explored interactions with cyanobacteria and environmental applications.
